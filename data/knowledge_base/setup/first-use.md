# First-use installation

## How do I install the purifier?
Place the unit upright on a stable, dry surface indoors. Leave at least 20 centimetres of clearance around air inlets and the outlet. Keep curtains and other loose material clear of the openings.

With the unit unplugged, open the rear cover, remove the cartridge, remove all plastic wrapping, and refit the cartridge and mesh pre-filter. Close the cover fully. Connect the supplied cable to a suitable socket matching the electrical rating on the rear label. Press Power once and select a fan speed.

## Does first use require an app?
No. Both models operate using physical controls. Wi-Fi pairing is optional and is supported only by Pure Plus. Aegis does not document outdoor use, bathroom installation, extension-cable specifications, or international plug compatibility in this demo. Do not infer those capabilities. If the cable is damaged or there is visible physical damage, leave the unit unplugged and contact support.
