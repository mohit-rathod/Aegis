# Pure Plus Wi-Fi pairing

## Which model supports Wi-Fi?
Only Pure Plus (AP-PLUS) supports app control. Pure Mini (AP-MINI) cannot be paired. Pure Plus supports a 2.4 GHz WPA2-Personal network; 5 GHz-only networks, enterprise authentication, and captive-portal networks are not supported in this demo.

## How do I pair Pure Plus?
Connect the phone to the intended 2.4 GHz network. In the fictional Aegis Home app, choose Add device, then Pure Plus. With the purifier powered on, hold the Wi-Fi button for five seconds until its light blinks blue. Follow the app prompts and enter the Wi-Fi password directly in the app.

A solid blue Wi-Fi light means connected. A blinking blue light means pairing. Pairing mode expires after two minutes; repeat the five-second hold if needed. The assistant must never request the Wi-Fi password. No public app-store URL is supplied for this fictional product. For an already paired device that is offline, use the connectivity troubleshooting guide.
