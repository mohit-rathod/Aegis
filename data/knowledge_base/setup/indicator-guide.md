# Indicator lights and filter reset

## What do the lights mean?
On both models, a steady amber filter light means the cartridge replacement reminder is due. It does not indicate a measured pollutant level or prove the cartridge is defective. After fitting a new compatible cartridge, power the unit on and hold Filter Reset for three seconds to clear the reminder.

A blinking red status light means the rear cover is not fully closed. Switch off and unplug the unit, reseat the cover, and restart. If the red light persists, stop using the unit and contact support.

## Which indicators are model-specific?
Pure Plus has a separate Wi-Fi light: blinking blue means pairing and solid blue means connected. A dark Wi-Fi light alone does not establish that the purifier has lost power. Pure Mini has no Wi-Fi light. Pure Plus Auto mode uses its particulate sensor; no numeric air-quality thresholds are specified here. For an undocumented light pattern or error code, request the model and observed pattern and escalate.
