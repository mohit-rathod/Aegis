# Troubleshooting noise and vibration

## Is every fan sound a fault?
Airflow noise normally increases at higher fan speeds. Sleep mode reduces speed and fan sound. Aegis does not publish decibel ratings in this demo, so the assistant must not quote a sound level.

## What can the customer check?
Switch off and unplug the unit. Place it upright on a stable, level surface. Check for loose external objects touching the housing and confirm that the cartridge is seated and its plastic wrapping has been removed. Close the rear cover fully before restarting.

Do not insert objects into the fan or remove screws. If there is scraping, persistent rattling, a burning smell, smoke, or unusual heat, stop using the unit, unplug it if safe, and contact support. Do not promise that continued operation is safe. Do not diagnose a motor failure from sound alone. Record the model and a description of the symptom; a recording is optional and should not include private conversation.
