# Troubleshooting an offline Pure Plus

## Does offline mean the purifier has stopped working?
No. A Pure Plus that has lost network connectivity can still be operated using its physical buttons. Pure Mini has no app connection and should not be diagnosed using this guide.

## How do I restore connectivity?
Check that the router is working and that the device is within range of its 2.4 GHz WPA2-Personal network. Restart the app. If needed, turn the purifier off, unplug it for 30 seconds, reconnect it, and turn it on. If the network name or password changed, repeat the Wi-Fi pairing procedure.

Do not ask the caller to disclose their Wi-Fi password, disable router security, or share a one-time code. An app outage cannot be confirmed from one offline device. This knowledge base contains no live service-status feed. If pairing still fails, collect the model, Wi-Fi light pattern, and approximate failure time, then escalate. Do not invent an outage announcement or a restoration deadline.
