# Troubleshooting weak airflow

## What should I check first?
Confirm that the purifier is on and check the selected mode. Sleep mode deliberately reduces fan speed. For a simple comparison, select manual speed 3 and observe whether airflow increases.

Switch off and unplug the unit before opening it. Check that the cartridge plastic wrapping was removed, that the rear cover is fully seated, and that the inlet and outlet have at least 20 centimetres of clearance. Clean the mesh pre-filter as described in the filter-care guide. Do not wash or vacuum the HEPA/carbon cartridge.

## When should support take over?
If airflow remains weak on speed 3 after these checks, record the model, approximate cartridge age, and indicator pattern, then contact human support. Do not ask the customer to open the motor housing or bypass the cover interlock. Weak airflow alone does not establish warranty coverage, a free replacement, or a refund. Aegis does not publish a home airflow measurement procedure in this demo.
