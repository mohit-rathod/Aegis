# Filter cleaning and replacement

## What can I clean?
Switch off and unplug the purifier before maintenance. Remove loose dust from the reusable mesh pre-filter with a soft brush or a vacuum on low suction. The pre-filter may be rinsed with plain water; allow it to dry completely before refitting. Do not operate the purifier with wet parts.

## What must not be washed?
The AF-M1 and AF-P1 cartridges contain HEPA and activated-carbon media. Do not wash, soak, or vacuum these cartridges. Replace a damaged cartridge or one with a persistent odour.

## When should I replace the cartridge?
Inspect it monthly. Six months is a typical planning interval, not a guaranteed service life; dust and usage can shorten it. The filter reminder counts operating time and does not directly measure remaining filtration capacity. Reset the reminder only after replacing the cartridge, using the indicator guide. Compatibility is model-specific: consult the filter-compatibility document.
