# Filter compatibility and accessories

## Which replacement filter fits my purifier?
Pure Mini (AP-MINI) uses replacement cartridge AF-M1. Pure Plus (AP-PLUS) uses replacement cartridge AF-P1. The cartridges are not interchangeable. Ask for the model code printed on the rear label before recommending a filter when the model is unclear.

## What is included?
Each purifier includes one fitted cartridge, a reusable mesh pre-filter, a power cable, and a quick-start sheet. Remove the cartridge packaging before first use. Replacement-cartridge packs contain the cartridge only; the reusable pre-filter stays with the purifier.

## Are third-party accessories supported?
Aegis has not validated third-party cartridges in this fictional catalogue. Do not promise equivalent performance or automatically declare the whole warranty void. Damage caused by an incompatible accessory is assessed under the warranty policy. Adapter kits, subscriptions, accessory prices, and availability are not documented. Offer human support for those questions rather than inventing an accessory or a purchase link.
