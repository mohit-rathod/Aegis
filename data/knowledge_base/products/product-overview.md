# Aegis Pure product overview

## Which models are available?
Aegis Pure Mini (AP-MINI) is a fictional tabletop air purifier for rooms up to 18 square metres. Aegis Pure Plus (AP-PLUS) is a fictional floor-standing model for rooms up to 35 square metres. These room-size recommendations assume a ceiling height of 2.7 metres. Aegis does not specify a clean-air delivery rate in this demo catalogue.

## What features differ?
Both models have three manual fan speeds, Sleep mode, and a filter reminder. Only Pure Plus has Auto mode, a particulate sensor, and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi app control. Pure Mini has no Wi-Fi or app support.

## What is outside the product scope?
Neither model measures carbon dioxide, provides ventilation, or has an ozone-generation mode. Do not describe either model as preventing illness or guaranteeing removal of all pollutants. No medical or sterilization claims are supported by this catalogue. Prices and stock availability require a separate current catalogue and are not provided here.
