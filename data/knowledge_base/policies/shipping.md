# Shipping and delivery policy

## Where does the fictional Aegis store deliver?
This demo store accepts direct orders to serviceable Indian postcodes only. Postcode serviceability must be confirmed by the order service or human support; a city name alone is insufficient.

## How long does delivery take?
Orders normally dispatch within two business days after payment confirmation. Standard transit is typically three to seven business days after dispatch. These are separate estimates, not a guaranteed delivery date. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays at the origin or destination. No holiday calendar is included in this dataset, so do not calculate an exact arrival date from these ranges.

## What costs and tracking are confirmed?
The shipping charge displayed at checkout is authoritative; no universal shipping fee or free-shipping threshold is documented. Order-specific status and estimated dates require the order-status tool. If tracking has not updated for three business days, offer human support investigation. Do not promise compensation, a replacement shipment, or delivery by a requested day without explicit authorization.
