# Limited warranty policy

## What is covered?
Pure Mini and Pure Plus have a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects from the purchase date on a valid invoice. Proof of purchase and the model or serial reference are required for review. This demo does not calculate exact warranty expiry timestamps; borderline dates require human confirmation.

## What is excluded?
Normal filter consumption, cosmetic wear, misuse, liquid damage, and damage caused by incompatible accessories are excluded from this limited warranty. A third-party accessory does not automatically void every unrelated claim. Cartridge defects reported on arrival may be reviewed; ordinary filter exhaustion is not itself a manufacturing defect.

## Who determines the remedy?
Human support assesses the evidence and determines an approved repair or replacement. The assistant may explain the policy and collect non-sensitive case details, but it cannot approve a warranty claim, promise a replacement date, or convert warranty coverage into a refund. Purchases from other sellers require invoice and seller verification. Escalate uncertain cases rather than inventing coverage.
