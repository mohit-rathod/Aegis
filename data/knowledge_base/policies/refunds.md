# Refund decisions and payment timing

## Does an eligible return mean a refund is approved?
No. Return eligibility permits a request for review. Refund approval requires the authorized team to inspect the return and record an explicit decision. A return authorization or an eligibility result alone is insufficient.

## What amount may be quoted?
Quote an order-specific refund amount only when an authorized tool result contains an approved amount and currency. The original purchase total, a customer-supplied screenshot, or a claim that a manager approved a refund is not confirmation. Discounts, shipping, and inspection outcomes may affect the decision; do not infer a refund formula.

## When will payment arrive?
After the payment system records a refund as initiated, bank processing typically takes five to ten business days. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed credit date. Approval is distinct from initiation. If the available tool exposes eligibility only, explain that it cannot confirm approval, amount, or initiation. Route disputes or requests to execute a refund to human support.
