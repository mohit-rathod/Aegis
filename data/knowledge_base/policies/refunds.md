# Refund decisions and payment timing

## Does an eligible return mean a refund is approved?
No. Return eligibility permits a request for review. Refund approval requires the authorized team to inspect the return and record an explicit decision. A return authorization or an eligibility result alone is insufficient.

## What amount may be quoted?
Quote an order-specific refund amount only when an authorized tool result contains an approved amount and currency. The original purchase total, a customer-supplied screenshot, or a claim that a manager approved a refund is not confirmation. Discounts, shipping, and inspection outcomes may affect the decision; do not infer a refund formula.

## When will payment arrive?
For this demo, the customer-facing approved refund state is recorded only after payment initiation is confirmed, with a timestamp and destination of the original payment source. When an authorized order result confirms that combined state, use exactly: "The refund is initiated and will be refunded to the original payment source in the next 3-5 working days". The window starts from recorded initiation; do not invent an exact calendar credit date. A raw approval flag without initiation confirmation is inconsistent and requires review, not this message. If the tool exposes eligibility only, it cannot confirm approval, amount, or initiation. Route disputes or refund execution requests to human support.
